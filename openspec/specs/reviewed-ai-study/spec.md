# reviewed-ai-study Specification

## Purpose
Prepare a reproducible and independently graded comparison of installed guidance without authorizing coding-agent execution.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Offline prepared reviewed AI comparison
Contributor tooling SHALL prepare a separate versioned feature evaluation comparing the same Rails/packs application with upstream guidance against installed Foundry guidance/checks, using existing Le Mans and independent grading. Six proposed serial trials SHALL use three repetitions per group and twenty-minute limits with matched conditions and workflow none. Inputs SHALL be frozen and evaluator resources isolated from candidates. Reference and faulty controls SHALL be verified offline before readiness is claimed.

#### Scenario: Study preparation
- **WHEN** the offline study preparation and controls pass
- **THEN** the protocol and frozen inputs are ready for separately authorized execution, with no agent/model/provider invocation performed
- **AND** reporting separates behavior, compliance, protocol, infrastructure, time and known usage without altering historical study outcomes
