## Why

Installed guidance remains customized and safe, but consuming new guidance requires manually comparing the whole catalog. Explicit reviewed upgrades should deliver unchanged-file improvements without erasing application decisions.

## What Changes

- Add assessment, digest-bound apply, explicit conflict resolution and conditional rollback to foundry:skills.
- Preserve template provenance, local edits and recoverable per-file progress outside skill discovery.
- Verify current eight-skill upgrades in installed consumers and execute upgraded verification helpers.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `reviewed-skill-upgrades`: safe assessment, reviewed application, recovery and provenance.

### Modified Capabilities
- `foundry-skills`: explicit reviewed upgrades complement preserving ordinary installation.

## Impact

Additive Rails generator options, development-only filesystem tooling, installed documentation and tests. No configuration schema, dependency, production runtime, catalog size or agent-study changes.
