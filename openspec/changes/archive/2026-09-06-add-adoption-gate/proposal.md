## Why

Gradual extraction needs a reviewed measure of existing debt without weakening strict package checks. Baselines must prevent both new violations and the return of resolved violations.

## What Changes

- Emit baseline drafts to stdout and compare current findings against explicit reviewed entries.
- Reject malformed exemptions and all non-boundary analysis failures.
- Keep strict commands unchanged and document adoption gate meaning.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `adoption-gate`: Exact, reviewed boundary debt comparison.

### Modified Capabilities
None.

## Impact

Adoption CLI and baseline validation, tests, installed guidance, and final compatibility evidence. No application writes or automated code/data migration.
