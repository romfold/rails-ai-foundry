## Purpose

Make the installed development kit sufficient to reproduce reviewed AI work with executable behavioral evidence.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Self-contained reviewed AI journey
The installed gem SHALL supply staged instructions for a PostgreSQL Rails-native ticket application with offline proposal generation, human review and durable local execution. Archive acceptance SHALL replay shipped instructions without requiring the source checkout in default and custom package roots, preserving workflow, customized skills, receipts and accepted debt.

#### Scenario: Installed consumer replay
- **WHEN** the shipped journey is followed in a fresh disposable consumer
- **THEN** HTTP, browser, queue and recovery acceptance verifies authorization, approval binding, revision safety and one committed local effect
- **AND** valid equivalent implementations pass while approval-bypass, revision-bypass, stale-generation and duplicate-execution controls fail behavioral assertions
