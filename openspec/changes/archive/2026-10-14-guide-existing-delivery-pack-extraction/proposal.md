## Why

Campfire's browser review established delivery parity but required investigation of route, helper, view and asset wiring. Installed guidance should make that extraction reproducible without importing Campfire's blocked test harness or changing its dependencies.

## What Changes

- Ship a staged guide for extracting existing Rails/Hotwire delivery while retaining shell models and data.
- Replay installed instructions against an ordinary Rails acceptance fixture with default/custom roots, HTTP/browser evidence and deliberate wiring/authorization/boundary faults.
- Add a conditional shared reference to existing skills and exercise reviewed upgrades preserving application customization.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `delivery-pack-extraction`: installed, behaviorally verified guidance for incremental Rails delivery extraction.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Documentation, existing skills, explicit packaging inventory and contributor acceptance. Eight skills, twelve applications, public CLI/configuration, dependency locks and application-owned execution remain unchanged. Campfire's blocked tasks, hosted release gate and historical studies stay open.
