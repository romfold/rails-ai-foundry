## Why

The twelve-app corpus is complete, but optional composition and experimental vectors lack executable recipes, adoption cannot yet reconcile complete debt identities, and evaluation/benchmark claims have no bounded evidence tooling. Complete the approved 0.5 milestone without introducing application execution runtimes.

## What Changes

- Add independently bundled ROM, dry-system and Trailblazer recipes with transactional outbox acceptance.
- Add experimental Pinecone and Weaviate recipes with explicit tenant/version boundaries and contract/service checks.
- Add disposable-copy debt auditing and application-specific Packwerk/pks parity evidence; preserve original baselines and keep Packwerk authoritative.
- Add offline evaluations, explicitly activated budgeted live evaluation tooling, and repeatable controlled benchmarks with held-out behavior checks.
- Record combinations, limits, CI, skills and version 0.5 after verification.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `advanced-composition`: Optional native composition recipes and atomic ROM outbox.
- `experimental-vectors`: Explicit experimental vector boundaries and acceptance.
- `adoption-evidence`: Read-only adoption inventory, disposable debt audit and parity evidence.
- `evaluation-evidence`: Offline/live evaluation guards and controlled benchmark evidence.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Existing Packwerk authority and production-runtime exclusions remain in force.

## Impact

Development CLI, independent recipe/evidence bundles, tests, CI, documentation and product skills. No new core production integration dependency. External providers remain opt-in; local completion does not claim live provider execution or hosted CI.
